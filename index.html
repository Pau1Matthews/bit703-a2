Aotearoa Adventure Gear

Aotearoa Adventure Gear was born from a simple belief: the rugged, breathtaking landscapes of New Zealand shouldn’t just be seen—they should be experienced deeply, safely, and sustainably. Founded by a group of passionate Kiwi outdoor enthusiasts, we started this journey because we couldn't find gear that truly matched the unpredictable, wild nature of our backyard. From the rain-swept peaks of the Southern Alps to the dense bush of Fiordland and the windswept coastlines of the North Island, Aotearoa demands gear that is as resilient as the land itself.
We don't just sell outdoor equipment; we curate and design gear built to withstand the unique elements of New Zealand. Our team tests every product right here in the elements, ensuring that whether you are embarking on a Great Walk, packing for a weekend tramping trip, or catching the morning surf, your gear will never let you down.
